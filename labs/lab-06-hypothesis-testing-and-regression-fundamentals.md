# Lab 06: Hypothesis Testing and Regression Fundamentals

## Phase

Analyze

## Objective

Interpret basic hypothesis testing, correlation, and regression concepts.

## Tasks

1. Write a practical null hypothesis and alternative hypothesis.
2. Choose an appropriate test conceptually for mean, proportion, or category comparisons.
3. Interpret p-value and confidence level from instructor-provided results.
4. Decide whether the result is practically meaningful.
5. Create or review a scatter plot.
6. Interpret correlation direction and strength.
7. Interpret a simple regression line.
8. Document what the analysis suggests for improvement.

## Deliverables

- Hypothesis testing worksheet
- Scatter plot or regression interpretation
- Analysis decision note

## Review Questions

1. What does a p-value help you decide?
2. Why is practical significance important?
3. Why does correlation not prove causation?

## Cleanup

Save conclusions for solution selection.
